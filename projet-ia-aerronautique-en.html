// METHODOLOGICAL & TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

This project, carried out by the collaborative team of Jimmy Dieux, Flavie Mielcarek, and Etienne Moyaux, is rigorously structured around four major technical axes aimed at transforming heterogeneous raw data into a high-performance decision-making tool.

1. Exploratory Analysis, Data Cleaning, and Feature Engineering

Anomaly handling, temporal variable structuring, and high-cardinality encoding

Missing data treatment: In-depth statistical analysis of the raw database to identify outliers. Flights with critical anomalies (mass cancellations, undocumented trajectory deviations, or chronological inconsistencies between departure and arrival) were discarded to preserve estimator consistency.

Feature Engineering: Extraction of temporal metadata from raw timestamps (day of the week, month, departure time slots, seasonal cyclicity) and calculation of theoretical flight duration to enhance algorithm sensitivity to cumulative delays.

Encoding and normalization: Application of One-Hot encoding on high-cardinality categorical variables (such as airline IDs and IATA codes for hundreds of origin and destination airports). Rigorous standardization of continuous variables to harmonize scales and optimize numerical convergence.

2. Deep Neural Network Modeling (Deep Learning)

Iterative design, sequential architectures, and regularization against overfitting

Incremental architecture approach: Progressive development of multilayer perceptron (MLP) neural network models. Initialization with a simple configuration (hidden layer of 64 neurons activated by a ReLU function) to establish a performance baseline.

Structural deepening: Scaling up to a deep network with 4 successive dense layers (128, 64, 32, and 16 neurons) to capture complex non-linearities in air traffic.

Regularization techniques: Integration of Batch Normalization layers to stabilize gradient descent at each epoch, combined with a dropout rate set at 20% to eliminate neuron co-adaptation and effectively counter overfitting. Despite hyperparameter optimization, this neural approach stabilized at an RMSE of 38.42 minutes, revealing the inherent complexity of tabular structures for purely neural architectures.

3. Advanced Optimization via Boosted Decision Trees (XGBoost)

Randomized hyperparameter search and superior performance on tabular data

Justification of algorithmic choice: Due to the tabular and sparse nature of flight data matrices, switching to the XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting) algorithm, recognized for its robustness against complex non-linear interactions.

Optimization via RandomizedSearchCV: Automated and stochastic exploration of key hyperparameter spaces (maximum tree depth, learning_rate, row and column subsampling) to maximize the model's generalization capacity.

Performance analysis: The optimized XGBoost model clearly outperformed the neural networks, drastically lowering the MSE to 27.44 and reducing the root mean squared error to a minimal RMSE of 5.24 minutes, thus validating the relevance of the tree-boosting approach for this aeronautical use case.

4. Ergonomics, Interface Design, and Application Prototyping

Translating mathematical models into an interactive, end-user-oriented tool

Operational constraints and input filtering: Voluntary restriction of interaction parameters to information accessible beforehand by a passenger or an airline prior to departure (airline, departure and arrival airports, scheduled times, calendar date).

Interactive interface prototyping: Development of a software overlay in the form of ergonomic widgets to simulate in real-time the predictive behavior of a flight (for example, the automated estimation of a potential 7.62-minute delay on a major transcontinental route between JFK and LAX operated by American Airlines).

// TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT & IMPROVEMENT PERSPECTIVES

This project successfully validated the entire lifecycle of an artificial intelligence project applied to the aeronautical sector, from database structuring to comparative model evaluation and ergonomic formatting of results.

🎯 Key Contributions & Achievements

  • ▸ Data pipeline mastery: Rigorous handling and automated cleaning of a massive flight dataset.
  • ▸ Comparative expertise: Methodical confrontation between Deep Learning (Keras/TensorFlow) and tabular Machine Learning (XGBoost).
  • ▸ Software valorization: Creation of a streamlined simulation interface demonstrating the operational exploitability of scores.

🚀 Evolution & Industrialization Avenues

  • ▸ Real-time meteorological coupling: Integration of weather API feeds to inject wind and visibility conditions live.
  • ▸ Multi-year extension: Broadening the training base over several years to capture macroeconomic trends.
  • ▸ Cloud-native deployment: Migration of the architecture to a containerized web application (Docker) accessible in production.
