// ASSOCIATIVE STEERING & STRATEGY

The association's presidency centered on a comprehensive revival of the structure to turn it into a true incubator for applied technical projects, divided into rigorous management axes.

1. Strategic & Operational Management

Role: Organizational structuring and legal compliance

Actions: Member recruitment and training (growth from 2 to 30 active members in 24 months), creation of internal processes (charter, roles, weekly meetings), and establishment of administrative and legal compliance (bylaws, SIREN number, prefecture and school filings).

Impact: A sustainable structure equipped with clear governance, ready to carry out ambitious, large-scale mechanical projects.

2. Financial Management & Sponsoring

Role: Fundraising and resource allocation

Actions: Management of a €10,000 annual budget, negotiation with 5 local partners securing a total funding of €3,000, and compilation/submission of public grant applications (City Hall, Region).

Impact: Optimal resource allocation across 3 simultaneous projects (4L Trophy, Group N Rally, Karting) closing with a balanced budget and a reinvested surplus.

3. Logistics & Workshops

Role: Organization of workspaces and inventory

Actions: Conducted a complete inventory of parts, consumables, and tools. Reorganization, storage optimization, and management of the association's stockroom.

Impact: Renovation, practical layout, and compliance upgrade of workshop premises for a safe and functional working environment.

// DEEP DIVE INTO THE 3 TECHNICAL PROJECTS

FLAGSHIP PROJECT 01

4L Trophy Preparation & Restoration

Mechanical restoration and complete preparation to tackle demanding desert tracks. The project required in-depth literature research and targeted optimization of vehicle reliability.

// TECHNICAL FOCUS AREAS:

  • Spare parts sourcing and study of manufacturer technical documentation.
  • Complete overhaul and reliability enhancement of the wiring harness to withstand vibrations & external conditions.
  • Major optimization of the cooling system against extreme heat.
  • Design and installation of a custom interior layout (storage, safety, survival gear).

Summary: Vehicle fully reliable and entry validated after 1.5 years of preparation work.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT 02

Citroën C2 VTS Rally

Transformation and preparation of a production vehicle in strict compliance with motorsport regulations (FFSA) to enter the association in rally and circuit events.

// TECHNICAL FOCUS AREAS:

  • Installation of homologated roll cage, bucket seats, and FIA harnesses.
  • Development of ground links (damper calibration and front geometry setup).
  • Global optimization of the braking system (discs, racing pads, braided brake lines).
  • Chassis weight reduction, complete engine/gearbox overhaul, and racing wiring harness adaptation.

Summary: A race-ready vehicle guaranteeing enhanced structural rigidity and dynamic performance.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT 03

Soapbox Design

Combining pure mechanical engineering (CAD design and manufacturing) with virtual numerical simulation, this project highlights the association's versatility between physical workshop and virtual training.

// TECHNICAL FOCUS AREAS:

  • CAD design and complete structural sizing (chassis, steering kinematics, braking).
  • Manufacturing, cutting, welding operations, and structural assembly in compliance with race regulations.
  • Execution of dynamic tests, tuning, and entry of the soapbox into local competition.

Summary: Successful participation in local races and creation of a seamless synergy between CAD modeling and track practice.
